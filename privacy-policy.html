BrainX - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026 | Effective for BrainX App & Website

Welcome to BrainX - Train • Think • Improve. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app hosted on GitHub Pages and published on Vivo App Store and other platforms.

1. Information We Collect

BrainX is a brain training game app. We respect your privacy and follow a minimal data collection approach:

  • Local Storage Only: Your game progress, XP, levels, and streaks are stored locally on your device using browser LocalStorage. We do not upload this data to our servers.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or exact location.
  • Device Info: For app performance, we may receive anonymous device type and OS version through GitHub Pages hosting.

2. Advertising (AdMob - Future Update)

Current version of BrainX on Vivo App Store does not contain ads. In future updates, we plan to integrate Google AdMob to support development:

  • AdMob may collect Advertising ID, IP address, and basic device info to show relevant ads.
  • Ads will not be shown on app launch or interrupt gameplay. Ads will only appear between levels.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID from device settings.
  • Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Policies

4. How We Use Information

  • To save and restore your game progress locally
  • To improve game difficulty and user experience
  • To maintain app stability and fix bugs
  • To communicate updates via our linked social channels (only if you join)

5. Data Security

Since we don't store personal data on servers, your data remains on your device. We use HTTPS (GitHub Pages) to protect data in transit. However, no method is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

BrainX is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us.

7. Permissions

Internet Access: Required to load the game from GitHub Pages and to open external links (Telegram/YouTube/Instagram).
Storage: Only to save your XP and levels locally on your device. No access to your photos or files.

8. Your Rights

  • You can clear app data anytime from device settings to reset progress
  • You can use BrainX without joining any social channels
  • You can request data deletion by contacting us (though we store nothing on server)

We may update this Privacy Policy when we add features like AdMob. The updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
